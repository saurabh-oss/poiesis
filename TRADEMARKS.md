# Name and logo

"Poiesis" and the Poiesis logo identify this project and its maintainer. The licences cover
the code, not the name.

You may:

- Say that your product or service uses, works with, or was built with Poiesis.
- Use the name to refer to this project in articles, talks and teaching.
- Keep the name in an unmodified copy you run inside your organisation.

You may not, without written permission:

- Use "Poiesis", or a confusingly similar name, as the name of a fork, product or service.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the maintainer endorses or runs your product
  or service.

A modified version you distribute must say it is modified and should use a different name.
Ask through the repository's issues if you are unsure.
