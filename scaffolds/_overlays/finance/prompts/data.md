
FINANCE DATA reads like finance when: amounts are uneven (4,812.60, not 5,000), spread over two
or three orders of magnitude, with most of the value in a few rows; dates fall on working days and
follow each other (raised, then approved a day or two later, then paid); references are sequences
with a prefix (`EXP-2026-0412`); a person named as requester or approver is one of the people who
sign in; and a row that refers to a supplier, a cost centre or an invoice refers to one that
exists (a `ref` to the library's table).
